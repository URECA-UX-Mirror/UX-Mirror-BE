## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sophisticated, curated sensibilities of contemporary Korean digital commerce—combining the editorial restraint of 29CM, the category precision of Musinsa, and the crisp daily-essential clarity of Market Kurly.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning modern consumers seeking curated fashion, lifestyle, and premium daily goods who value deliberate visual quietude over aggressive sales noise.
- **Brand Personality:** Curated, serene, trustworthy, and architectural. The product prioritizes high-fidelity photography, expansive whitespace, and refined micro-typography.
- **Design Movement:** Minimalist Editorial Modernism with quiet tactile precision. Visual authority is asserted through meticulous typographic cadence, razor-thin structured dividers (1px), and an intentional absence of gratuitous drop shadows or skeuomorphic decorations.

## Colors

The palette is deliberately monochrome-dominant with a high-clarity botanical mint accent used strictly for navigational indicators, verification badges, stock availability, and primary checkout conversions.

- **Primary Neutral (`#111111`):** Deep solid charcoal utilized for primary branding, expressive display headlines, active tab labels, and primary button fills. Replaces pure `#000000` to prevent visual fatigue while retaining high-contrast authority.
- **Secondary Accent (`#00a389`):** Refined sage/mint calibrated for high visibility against white backdrops. Reserved for critical interactive highlights, discount ratios (`30%`), and active state accents.
- **Tertiary Soft Mint (`#e6f7f3`):** Delicate mint tint applied to micro-badges, member-only benefit chips, free shipping alerts, and subtle focus halos.
- **Neutral Hierarchy:**
  - Base Backgrounds: `#FFFFFF` (Canvas Pure) and `#F8F9FA` (Subtle Module Canvas).
  - Borders & Structure: `#EEEEEE` (Hairline rules) and `#E5E7EB` (Interactive boundaries).
  - Secondary Text: `#4A4A4A` (Body text) and `#8E8E93` (Metadata, SKUs, and utility labels).

## Typography

Typography bridges structured geometric Latin numerals with Korean Hangul script legibility.

- **Display & Numerical Elements:** Utilizes `Plus Jakarta Sans` for bold editorial impact, Western numerals, currency values (e.g., `48,000원`), and English editorial titles. Tight kerning (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates a disciplined, bespoke magazine feel.
- **Hangul Body & Content:** Utilizes `Noto Sans` (with Pretendard fallback) to ensure exceptional legibility across CJK characters. Hangul copy avoids extreme thin weights, standardizing on Regular (`400`) and SemiBold (`600`) to maintain structural baseline weight against product images.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Korean titles default to `headline-md` and `headline-sm`. Brand badges and product taglines use uppercase Latin labels with expanded tracking (`0.04em`) to establish visual separation.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout adheres to an architectural 12-column grid optimized for 1440px desktop resolutions, constrained to a maximum content width of 1360px with generous vertical cadence.

### Layout Rules
- **Grid Architecture:** 12 equal columns with `gutter-desktop` (24px) and outer gutters bounded by `margin-desktop` (80px). Product grids reflow deliberately into 4-column (280px cards), 3-column (380px cards), or 2-column editorial split configurations.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major marketing and editorial collection blocks maintain `space-3xl` (96px) top and bottom margins to preserve breathing room between dense product matrices.
- **Dividers & Boundaries:** Content modules are isolated through structural white space and 1px hairlines (`#EEEEEE`) rather than heavy container cards.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system operates on a zero-shadow, flat-editorial depth model. Visual depth is established through tonal layering, stark white canvas isolation, and crisp border boundaries.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` ground. Alternating contextual modules employ `#F8F9FA` to define full-width editorial zones without heavy borders.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Flat `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px solid `#EEEEEE` structural perimeter. No box shadow is applied in default states.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interaction):** Elevated cards retain flat geometry; depth is represented by border color shift from `#EEEEEE` to `#111111` or `#00A389`, accompanied by subtle image micro-scaling (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Level 3 (Fixed Overlays & Dropdowns):** Global header, filter trays, and search modals utilize a high-density backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with a 92% opaque white layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) and a hairline bottom edge (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Deep utility drawers apply a faint ambient occlusion tint (`0 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) enclosed by a 1px solid border (`#E5E7EB`).

## Shapes

The design language favors crisp, architectural geometry. Soft micro-radii provide modern warmth while resisting the overly playful, bubble-like appearance of mass-market apps.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to standard form inputs, utilitarian buttons, product image containers, and contextual chips.
- **Accent Badges & Status Tags (0.125rem / 2px or 9999px Pill):** Small notification markers and percentage discount tags use a sharp 2px radius; utility filter chips optionally use full pill geometry to denote toggle behavior.
- **Image Frames:** Product thumbnails strictly feature subtle 4px corner radii with `overflow: hidden` to prevent clipping visual artifacts while retaining edge sharpness.

## Components

### Global Header & Navigation
- **Top Utility Bar:** 36px height, text size `body-sm` (`#8E8E93`), containing membership tiers, login, order inquiry, and localized language switchers.
- **Main Sticky Bar:** 72px height, `#FFFFFF` with 92% blur. Houses the wordmark on the left, primary navigation links horizontally centered, and search/cart/mypage icons on the right.
- **Navigation Links:** `[베스트, 신상품, 이용정보, 고객센터, 마이페이지]` rendered in `label-lg` (`#222222`). Active state features a persistent 2px underline in `#111111` or `#00a389`.
- **Search Utility:** Inline expandable input with a clean 1px border (`#EEEEEE`), placeholder text in `#8E8E93`, and a minimalist search glyph.

### Product Card
- **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 3:4 vertical ratio to favor modern fashion, beauty, and culinary staging.
- **Image Container:** Background `#F8F9FA`, 4px corner radius, hairline 1px inner border. Hover triggers subtle zoom transition (`duration-300`).
- **Badge Placement:** Upper-left corner displays discount (`30%` in `#00A389`, font `numeric-price`) or status (`BEST`, `EXCLUSIVE` in `#111111` solid badge with white text).
- **Metadata Stack:** Brand name (`label-sm`, `#8E8E93`, uppercase), product title (`body-md`, `#222222`, 2-line clamp), followed by price grouping with final price (`numeric-price`, `#111111`) and original strikethrough price (`body-sm`, `#8E8E93`).

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#111111` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 48px height, 4px radius, `label-lg`. Active hover shifts to `#2E2E2E`.
- **Accent Action (Checkout / Immediate Buy):** Solid `#00A389` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#008B74`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px border (`#111111`), text `#111111`.
- **Ghost Utility:** Borderless, 36px height, text `#6B7280`, hover text `#111111` with `#F8F9FA` background fill.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Height: 36px. Default state features a 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), white background, and text `#4A4A4A`.
- Active state transitions to `#111111` background with white text, or `#E6F7F3` background with `#00A389` text and border for multi-select facet groups.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height: 48px. 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border, 4px radius, interior horizontal padding `space-md` (16px).
- Focus state switches border to 1px `#111111` with a zero-offset outline or subtle `#E6F7F3` halo.
- Error state shifts border to `#E11D48` with helper text rendered in `body-sm`.

### Checkbox & Radio
- Size: 18x18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio). Default border 1.5px `#D1D5DB`. Checked state fills `#111111` or `#00A389` with a crisp centered white checkmark.
